Stabletrust Pro is a confidential, multi-approval treasury dashboard for teams, businesses, and institutions. A team shares a single on-chain wallet whose balances and payout amounts stay encrypted on-chain, and whose spends require approval from multiple members.

Stabletrust Pro includes:

- Confidential single and batch payouts
- Configurable N-of-M approval thresholds for every spend
- Encrypted balances and amounts, decryptable only by the team
- Passwordless sign-in (email or Google) and email-based team invites

<Info>
  **No seed phrases, no extensions.** Members sign in with email or Google. The Organisation's wallet is held in a secure enclave.
</Info>

## What you can do

- Pay contributors, vendors, or payroll without leaking amounts or balances
- Require multiple approvals before any payout leaves the treasury
- Fund the treasury with any single admin, while spends stay multi-signature
- Manage your team: invite members, assign roles, and change the approval threshold

## How it works

Stabletrust Pro combines two independent layers:

| Layer | Provider | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signing & approvals | Turnkey secure enclaves | Custody of the treasury wallet, N-of-M approval policies, passwordless auth |
| Confidential settlement | Fairblock | Encrypted balances and transfers  |

Each treasury is an isolated Turnkey sub-organization holding one shared wallet. Turnkey never sees transaction amounts (they are encrypted before signing), and Fairblock never holds keys. For the developer-level integration, see the [Turnkey API integration](/ct/guides/build/api-integrations/turnkey) guide.

## Approval model

Approvals are enforced by policy at signing time, so a payout cannot be broadcast until enough members approve it.

| Action | Approvals required |
| --- | --- |
| Deposit / add funds | Any single admin (1-of-M) |
| Send a payout (transfer or withdrawal) | Team threshold (N-of-M) |
| View or decrypt balances and amounts | Any single admin (1-of-M) |

The threshold - for example 2-of-3, is chosen by the Owner of the Organisation and can be changed in Settings.

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Stabletrust Pro">
    Launch the [dashboard](https://pro.stabletrust.io) and sign in with email or Google.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your treasury">
    Name the organization and set your approval threshold (for example 2-of-3).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Add members by email; each signs in passwordlessly and can approve payouts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund and pay">
    Deposit, then send single or batch confidential payouts for the team to approve.
  </Step>
</Steps>
